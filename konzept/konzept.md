Context
Official documents are frequently written in dense, technical language that 
excludes a significant portion of the population — including non-native 
speakers, older adults, and people with low literacy. This creates 
unnecessary barriers to healthcare, public services, and legal rights that 
plain-language translation could readily remove.

Task
A mobile app where users photograph any official document — such as a 
government letter, medical form, or medication leaflet. One AI agent 
translates the content into plain, accessible language, while a second 
agent provides clear, actionable next steps. Text-to-speech output and 
multilingual support make the app a practical inclusion tool for people with 
language barriers, older adults, or anyone overwhelmed by complex 
official communication.
